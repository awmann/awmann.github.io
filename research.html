Major Projects from the Young Worlds Lab

We study how planets change in their first few hundred million years. That means finding planets around young stars, measuring how old those stars are, and comparing young planets to the mature ones found by Kepler and TESS. Each project below tackles one piece of that.

More results

  • The Featherweight Giant: JWST shows the 17 Myr planet HIP 67522 b is Jupiter-sized but only about 14 Earth masses.
  • TOI-1224 and MELANGE-5: two young planets with transit timing variations in a newly found association.
  • IRAS 04125+2902b (TIDYE-1b): a 3 Myr transiting planet with a misaligned disk.
  • The Greater Pleiades Complex: the Pleiades is the core of a much larger, dissolving structure.

Code & data

Code

  • MOLUSC: limits on unseen stellar companions from radial velocities, high-resolution imaging, and Gaia (more).
  • EVA: association ages from Gaia excess photometric uncertainties (more).
  • Comove: finds comoving neighbors of a target star and returns youth diagnostics (FindFriends), and tests whether a close neighbor is a binary companion or a chance alignment (BinProb).

Catalogs

  • TESS All-Sky Rotation Survey (TARS): rotation periods, light curves, and periodograms at the MAST high-level science product page and on Zenodo (more).

Data tables and spectra from older papers

  • Spectro-Thermometry of M dwarfs and their candidate planets (arXiv:1311.0003): spectra of habitable-zone candidate hosts, other KOIs, and calibrators (ReadMe); FITS versions in HZC.zip, KOIs.zip, and Calibrators.zip (ReadMe); tables of stellar and planet parameters (also here and here as plain ASCII).
  • How to Constrain Your M Dwarf (arXiv:1501.01635): empirical parameters and synthetic photometry.
  • Testing the Metal of Late-Type Kepler Planet Hosts with Iron-Clad Methods (arXiv:1304.7269): KOI and non-KOI stellar parameters.
  • Prospecting in late-type dwarfs (arXiv:1211.4630): wide binary sample and metal-sensitive features.
  • They Might Be Giants (arXiv:1202.5394): parameters of observed Kepler targets.
  • Revised Filter Profiles and Zero Points for Broadband Photometry (arXiv:1412.1474): filter profiles.
  • Other files: spectral template library (640 templates) and LaTeX tables of KOI and non-KOI stellar parameters.
